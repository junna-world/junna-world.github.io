Help

How long do eggs take? Common 2 minutes, Rare 20 minutes, Epic 2 hours, Legendary 8 hours, Mythic 12 hours. An egg's rarity stays hidden until you crack it open. Tap an egg to warm it (up to a tenth of its time), watch an ad to cut an hour, or use gems to hatch it now.

What are the odds? Tap the ⓘ on any egg in the Shop to see the chance of each rarity. If you buy 12 Ancient Eggs without a Mythic dragon, the 12th is guaranteed Mythic.

I got the same dragon again. Duplicates level up the dragon you already have. Fully grown dragons give gold and gems instead.

Too many notifications? Settings (gear icon) › Notifications lets you turn off egg, treasure and daily reminders one by one.

Restore purchases: Settings (gear icon) › Restore Purchases. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and in your iCloud (when signed in), so reinstalling or moving to a new iPhone keeps it.

Cancel Keeper Club: iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
