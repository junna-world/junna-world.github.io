Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Third-Rate Swordsman (삼류검객) is made by ZZUNDI studio. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect your personal data ourselves. Some data is handled by Apple and by our advertising partner, Google, as described below.

On your device

Your game progress (cleared stages, Gold Ingots, training, unlocked techniques and warriors, sword skins, settings and runs played) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you don't have the Martial Alliance Patron subscription, the game shows an ad before every second run using Google AdMob. You can also choose to watch a reward video to double a run's Gold Ingots or to get free Gold Ingots once a day. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time in the Shop. On iOS, you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your fastest clear time for each stage is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center information.

Purchases

Gold Ingot packs, bundles and the Martial Alliance Patron subscription are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

Children

Third-Rate Swordsman isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.
