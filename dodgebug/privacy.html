Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Dodgebug does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • Game progress (best score, level, coins, upgrades, sound setting) is stored only on your device and never leaves it.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Game Center information.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
