FAQ

How do I move? Drag anywhere on the screen. The ladybug follows your finger's motion, so your finger never covers it.

Where did my progress go? Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I turn off sound? Tap the speaker on the main menu, or use your phone's silent switch.
