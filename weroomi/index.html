Weroomi

A simple calendar to share with family, a partner or roommates.

FAQ

How do I invite someone? Tap ⋯ at the top › Room Settings › "Send Invite Link", and send the link in KakaoTalk, Messages or any app. When they open it on an iPhone, they join the room.

Do I need an account? No. Weroomi uses your iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud in Settings.

Can Android users join? Not yet. Weroomi is iPhone only for now.

Can I remove someone from a room? Rooms are shared by link, so anyone with it can join. If the wrong person joined, the room's maker can delete the room and make a new one.

How do I leave a room? Tap ⋯ at the top › Room Settings › "Leave Room". The room's events disappear from your iPhone.
