Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Weroomi is made by ZZUNDI studio. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect your calendar or personal data ourselves. Some data is handled by Apple and by our advertising partner, Google, as described below.

Your calendar

Your rooms, events and the name you use in each room are stored on your iPhone and in iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit. A room lives in the iCloud of the person who made it. We can't see any of it.

Sharing a room

When you share a room, the people who open your invite link can see and change that room's events and see the names people use in it. Anyone who has the link can join while the link is on, so send it only to people you trust. The room's owner can remove people and turn the link off. Shared data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Event alerts, and alerts when someone adds an event, are created on your iPhone. You can turn them off in Weroomi's settings or in iPhone Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have Ad-Free, Weroomi shows a small banner ad using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time. On iOS, you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Ad-Free is a monthly subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Children

Weroomi isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.
