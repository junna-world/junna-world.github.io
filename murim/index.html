FAQ

How do I move? Touch anywhere and drag; the stick appears under your finger. Attacks are automatic. Tap the round button at the bottom right to dash.

What do the red areas on the ground mean? Every enemy attack is shown as a red area first. When it fills up, it hits. Step out, dash through, or kill the attacker first.

Where did my progress go? Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I restore purchases? Shop › Restore Purchases.
