Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Dodgebug is made by ZZUNDI studio. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect your personal data ourselves. Some data is handled by Apple and by our advertising partner, Google, as described below.

On your device

Your game progress (best score, level, Dewdrops, item upgrades, settings and games played) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you don't have the Ad-Free subscription, Dodgebug shows ads between runs using Google AdMob. You can also choose to watch a reward video once a day for a free Dewdrop. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time. On iOS, you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Dewdrop packs and the Ad-Free subscription are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best score is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center information.

Children

Dodgebug isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.
