Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Drakeling is made by ZZUNDI studio. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect your personal data ourselves. Some data is handled by Apple and by our advertising partner, Google, as described below.

On your device

Your game progress (eggs, dragons, gold, gems, Keeper Rank and settings) is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage so you can continue on a new iPhone. We can't see or access it. To erase it, delete the app and remove Drakeling's data in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Drakeling schedules local reminders on your device when an egg is ready to hatch, your treasure is full, or your daily reward is waiting. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off anytime in iPhone Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Drakeling only shows video ads when you choose to watch one for a reward (a shorter hatch time, free gems or double treasure). Keeper Club members get these rewards without ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices inside the app. On iOS, you can also allow or deny tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Gem packs and the Keeper Club subscription are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

Children

Drakeling isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.
